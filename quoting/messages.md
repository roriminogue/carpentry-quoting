# Client messages

Wording for ServiceM8's **Automation** add-on and for messages you send by hand. Keep SMS under ~160 characters where possible (longer ones cost 2 SMS credits). Replace `[square brackets]`. ServiceM8's own merge fields (client name, quote link and so on) can replace the `{curly}` placeholders; pick them from its "insert field" menu.

---

### 1. Instant reply to a new enquiry (SMS, automatic)

> Hi {first name}, thanks for getting in touch with [BUSINESS NAME]. I've got your request and will call you within 1 business day to chat or book a measure-up. – [FIRST NAME]

### 2. Booking the measure-up (SMS, sent from the job)

> Hi {first name}, I'll come out to measure up on {date} at {time}. Reply YES to confirm or let me know a better time. – [FIRST NAME], [BUSINESS NAME]

### 3. Quote sent (email, with the quote attached / link)

> **Subject:** Your quote from [BUSINESS NAME]: {job description}
>
> Hi {first name},
>
> Thanks for having me out. Your quote is attached. You can accept it online with the button below, and I'll send through a 10% deposit request and lock in a start date.
>
> The quote is valid for 30 days. If you have any questions or want to change anything, just reply or call me on [MOBILE].
>
> Cheers,
> [FIRST NAME]
> [BUSINESS NAME] · Licence [LICENCE NO.]

### 4. Follow-up: 2 days after quote (SMS, automatic)

> Hi {first name}, just checking you got the quote for your {job type}. Happy to answer any questions. – [FIRST NAME]

### 5. Follow-up: 7 days after quote (email, automatic)

> **Subject:** Any questions about your quote?
>
> Hi {first name}, I wanted to check in on the quote I sent for {job description}. If the price or scope isn't quite right, let me know. There's often a way to adjust it (different boards, staging the work, etc.). My next available start is around [MONTH].
>
> Cheers, [FIRST NAME]

### 6. Quote expiring: day 25 (SMS, automatic)

> Hi {first name}, your quote for the {job type} expires in 5 days. After that I'll need to re-check timber prices. Let me know if you'd like to go ahead. – [FIRST NAME]

### 7. Job finished: review request (SMS, after the invoice is paid)

> Thanks again {first name}! If you're happy with the {job type}, a quick Google review would really help a small local business: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK] – [FIRST NAME]

Get the review link from Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews".

### 8. Declining a job politely (by hand)

> Hi {first name}, thanks for thinking of me. Unfortunately this one isn't a good fit for me right now ([reason: booked out / outside my area / needs a specialist]). [I'd suggest NAME on PHONE.] All the best with it. – [FIRST NAME]
