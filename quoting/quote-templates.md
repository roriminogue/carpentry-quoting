# Quote templates

Copy these into ServiceM8 **job templates** and the **quote document footer**. Replace everything in `[square brackets]`.

---

## Quote footer: terms (paste into every quote)

```
[BUSINESS NAME] · ABN [ABN] · NSW Contractor Licence [LICENCE NO.]

1. This quote is valid for 30 days. Timber prices change often; after 30 days we'll re-check material prices before confirming.
2. Prices include GST.
3. Deposit: 10% on acceptance (the NSW maximum). The balance is due on completion, or as progress payments for larger jobs. Invoices are due within 7 days.
4. Jobs over $5,000 (incl. GST) need a signed written contract before work starts. Jobs over $20,000 also need Home Building Compensation cover, which we arrange before taking any money.
5. The quote covers only the work described. Extra work or changes are variations, agreed and priced in writing before they're done.
6. Unless stated otherwise, the price doesn't include painting, council or certifier fees, electrical or plumbing work, or fixing hidden problems found once work starts (rot, termite damage, out-of-level structure). We'll show you and price anything like this before going ahead.
7. Rubbish removal is included only where listed.
8. Materials remain our property until paid for in full.
```

## Templates by job type

### 1. Deck: new build

**Description (pre-filled):**
```
Supply and build a [L] m x [W] m [BOARD TYPE] deck, approx. [AREA] m², [HEIGHT] mm off the ground.
- Set out and footings (concrete/stirrups) to suit site
- H4/H3 treated pine bearers and joists at 450 mm centres, built to Australian standards
- [BOARD TYPE] decking boards, [screw-fixed / hidden-fixed]
- [STEPS] steps to [garden/side]
- [BALUSTRADE m] lin m timber balustrade (required where the deck is over 1 m high)
- [Sand and oil / left raw to weather before oiling]
- All site waste removed
```
**Items:** DECK-FRAME (m²), DECK-BOARD (m²), DECK-STEP, BALUSTRADE, MATERIALS, WASTE

**Before quoting, check:** height and area (over 1 m high or over 25 m² usually means council/certifier approval), access for materials, slope, existing services under the deck area. Use `quote-calculator.xlsx` for the numbers.

### 2. Deck: re-board or repair

```
Remove existing decking boards from approx. [AREA] m² deck and dispose of them.
Inspect frame; replace up to [X] lin m of joists if damaged (more priced as a variation).
Supply and lay new [BOARD TYPE] boards. [Sand and oil.]
```
**Items:** DECK-REBOARD (m²), MATERIALS, DECK-OIL, WASTE

### 3. Doors and trims

```
[Number] x [internal/external] doors: [remove old, supply and install prehung / hang supplied door].
[Fit handles/locksets supplied by client / supplied by us: model].
[Replace architraves / skirting in: rooms].
Doors are left ready for painting (painting not included).
```
**Items:** DOOR-PREHUNG / DOOR-HANG / DOOR-EXT, LOCKSET, ARCH-SET, SKIRT, MATERIALS

### 4. General carpentry / small renovation (hourly or fixed)

For jobs that are hard to price per unit, quote a fixed price built from estimated hours, but write the scope tightly:

```
Scope:
- [Task 1]
- [Task 2]
Estimated [X] hours on site plus materials.
Not included: [painting, electrical, plumbing, tiling, ...].
```
**Items:** LAB-HR × hours (or LAB-DAY), MATERIALS, WASTE

Tip: for open-ended repair work, offer **"$90/hr + materials, estimated $X–$Y"** in writing instead of a fixed price, and update the client if it looks like going over.

## Pricing rules of thumb (starting points: adjust to your actual costs)

- **Labour:** $90/hr ex GST ($99 inc). Sydney carpenters typically charge $63–$150/hr inc GST, about $98 on average. ([source](https://whatsthedamage.com.au/carpenter-cost-sydney/))
- **Materials:** supplier price + 15% (covers pick-up, wastage and the risk of price changes).
- **Minimum charge:** 2 hours. Small jobs still cost travel and setup time.
- **Contingency:** add 10% to old houses and any job where you can't see the structure.
- **Price check:** after each job, compare actual hours with quoted hours and adjust the rates in the price list and calculator.
